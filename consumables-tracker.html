Restock/transfer
Fill in a row per item — every field is required. Set "From" to a location to transfer stock out of it (deducted from that location, "Received from" locks to N/A); set "From" to "External / supplier" to receive new stock instead (pick a "Received from" value, plus a Lot/Expiry if you want that batch tracked). Blank rows are ignored. Note: transfers here move quantity only — to link a transfer to a specific tracked batch, use the item card's own Transfer button instead.

Daily update
One row per reagent, like the paper consumption sheet: Balance is the current count, In is what was received today — type a source under the quantity; if it matches one of your locations exactly (e.g. "PATHO"), that quantity is actually pulled from that location's stock instead of just logged as an external receipt. Out is what was consumed, Transfer moves stock out to another location (pick the destination in "To"). Leave a field blank to skip it.
